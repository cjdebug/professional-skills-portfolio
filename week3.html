Interview Skills

This week focused on developing the skills needed to perform confidently and professionally in job interviews. We learned about the importance of effective communication, including the 7 C's of communication and the STAR method for organising interview answers. During the session, we also watched interview videos and discussed the important do's and don'ts to follow when attending an interview.

Job Interview

A job interview is a formal conversation between an employer and a candidate. It allows the employer to evaluate the candidate's skills, experience, attitude and suitability for the position, while giving the candidate an opportunity to learn more about the job and its responsibilities.

Communication at an interview

Non-verbal communication

Body language, eye contact, posture and facial expressions that show confidence and interest.

Listening

Paying full attention so you understand each question before answering.

Responding

Giving clear, relevant and honest answers.

Questioning

Asking thoughtful questions to show interest in the role and the company.

The 7 C's of effective communication

Clear

Present information clearly.

Concise

Don't lose the message by being long-winded.

Correct

Avoid misleading information.

Complete

Give all the necessary information, not just part of it.

Concrete

Use examples and stories rather than generalities.

Courteous

Always be and sound polite.

Confident

Show a confident tone and a “can do” attitude.

The STAR method

StepWhat to say
S · SituationGive brief details of the situation.
T · TargetSay what you wanted to achieve.
A · ActionSay what you did.
R · ResultSay what the result was.

Tip: signpost your evidence, for example “I'm going to tell you about a time when…”.

Activity: do's and don'ts of an interview

Do

  • Research the company and the role beforehand
  • Dress smartly and professionally
  • Keep eye contact and sit with good posture
  • Listen carefully and answer with clear examples
  • Thank the interviewer and follow up

Don't

  • Arrive late or unprepared
  • Dress too casually
  • Slouch, fidget, or use your phone
  • Give vague or long-winded answers
  • Lie or exaggerate your experience
