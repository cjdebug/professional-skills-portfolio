# Professional Skills Portfolio

My personal learning portfolio developed for the Professional Skills (IT1215) module at SLIIT City Uni.

## About the Portfolio

This portfolio documents my learning journey throughout the Professional Skills module. It includes weekly session summaries, key concepts, practical activities, personal reflections, and skills developed during the course.

## Topics Covered

- Introduction to Professional Skills
- Portfolio Writing and Paper Craft Challenge
- Interview Skills
- CV Writing
- Emotional Intelligence
- Building a Personal Brand
- Professional Etiquette in Communication
- Dining Etiquette
- Negotiation Skills

## Group Project

**Zoo Awareness Programme - National Zoological Gardens, Dehiwala**

An educational awareness programme conducted on 17 October 2026 to promote wildlife protection and conservation through interactive games, quizzes and activities for visitors.

## Purpose

The purpose of this portfolio is to reflect on my personal and professional development while demonstrating the communication, teamwork, leadership, problem-solving and other transferable skills I have gained throughout the module.
