Emotional Intelligence

This week focused on understanding emotions and the importance of emotional intelligence in our daily lives. We learned about different types of emotions, how they are classified and how they influence our thoughts and behaviour. The session also introduced Goleman's five emotional competencies and explored different ways to manage and control our emotions effectively.

Understanding Emotions

An emotion is more than just a feeling. It involves different aspects of how we think, feel and behave.

  • Feelings: The emotions we experience, such as happiness, sadness or fear.
  • Thoughts: The ideas and reactions that come to our mind when experiencing an emotion.
  • Mind and Body Responses: The psychological and biological changes that occur when we feel something.
  • Behaviour: The way we express our emotions through our actions and reactions.

Emotional leakage: the unintentional revealing of our real feelings through facial expressions, tone of voice or body language.

What is emotional intelligence?

“The ability to monitor one's own and other people's emotions, to discriminate between different emotions and label them appropriately, and to use emotional information to guide thinking and behaviour.”Salovey and Mayer, 1990

Goleman's five emotional competencies

Self-awareness

Knowing what emotions you feel.

Self-regulation

Managing your emotions and reactions.

Motivation

Using your emotions to work toward goals.

Empathy

Understanding other people's feelings.

Social skills

Handling relationships well.

Self-regulation strategies

Mindfulness

Awareness that arises from paying attention, on purpose, in the present moment and non-judgementally (Jon Kabat-Zinn).

Cognitive re-appraisal

Looking at a situation in a new way.

Acceptance

Accepting feelings without fighting them.

Problem solving

Acting on the cause of the emotion.
