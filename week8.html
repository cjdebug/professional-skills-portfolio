The Office Survival Challenge

Objective

To develop professional communication, negotiation, teamwork, decision-making and problem-solving skills in a realistic workplace scenario.

Materials needed

  • Printed scenario handouts (one per group)
  • Printed item list (see below)
  • One answer sheet per group
  • Pens

Scenario

Your team is a department in a corporate office building on the 12th floor. Suddenly, a massive blackout and system failure occur, and you're told to evacuate immediately. The elevators aren't working, and there's a potential fire risk.

You only have 15 minutes to gather 7 items from your department and head down safely while maintaining communication with your head office and clients using professional skills.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Divide the class into groups of 5–6 students.
  2. Hand out the scenario and the item list (15 items total).
  3. Ask them to discuss and choose ONLY 7 items they will take with them. Every item must be justified with a professional reason (e.g., laptop: communication; first aid kit: health and safety).
  4. Each team fills in the answer sheet, listing:
    • The 7 selected items
    • The reason for each (professional relevance)
    • The role each group member played (e.g., leader, recorder, timekeeper, communicator)
  5. Allow 15–20 minutes for discussion and completion.
  6. Ask each group to briefly present their choices (optional, based on time).

Sample item list

  1. Laptop with client database
  2. Company first aid kit
