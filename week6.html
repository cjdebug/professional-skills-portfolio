Building a Personal Brand

This week focused on understanding personal branding and how it influences the way others see us. We learned that a personal brand is built through our behaviour, skills, values and the impression we create. The session also explored how to develop a strong personal brand, both in everyday interactions and on social media. I understood that if we do not actively shape our personal brand, others may form their own impressions of us.

Understanding Personal Branding

A personal brand represents how others see and remember us. It is shaped by our personality, behaviour, values and the impression we leave on people. The way we present ourselves influences how others perceive us.

Key points in personal branding

Tell a story

Let people see who you are and where you're going.

Have structure

Present yourself in an organised, recognisable way.

Be consistent

Use the same message and style everywhere.

Be authentic

Stay true to yourself.

Focus

Concentrate on a clear area, not everything.

Personalize

Make it specific to you and your audience.

What I Learned

Through this session, I learned that building a personal brand is something we need to work on consciously, as the way we behave and present ourselves influences how others see us. I understood that being consistent, genuine and respectful helps create a positive impression and build trust with others. This session also encouraged me to think about how I would like people to describe me when I am not around. I want to be known as someone who is friendly, dependable and easy to work with. Moving forward, I plan to pay more attention to my behaviour, how I communicate and how I treat others. I also want to maintain a positive and authentic image, both in person and on social media.
